# Source provenance

The face, bundled brown hair, articulated hands and common humanoid rig derive from the same audited **legacy HairSample_Male** CC0 asset used by work order 001. It is specifically listed among the CC0 models by pixiv; current VRoidPreset_A–Z models are not substituted. The existing free Quaternius animation subset supplies the original motion base. Class-specific outfit meshes, armor, accessories, weapons, atlas palette edits, weapon attack/skill poses and attachment animation are authored for this project. No additional third-party models, paid assets or paid 3D generation services were used.

Each class `sources.json` records the upstream asset hashes, primary licensing URLs, project-original work and portrait-reference hash. The existing user-designated portraits are project design references; they are not relabeled CC0 and are not projected onto the meshes.

Primary source pages checked on 2026-11-09:
- pixiv's sample-model conditions identify `HairSample_Male` under CC0: https://vroid.pixiv.help/hc/en-us/articles/4402614652569
- Quaternius Universal Animation Library identifies CC0 and personal/commercial use: https://quaternius.com/packs/universalanimationlibrary.html

CC0 license text is retained in `generation/CC0-1.0.txt`. Existing source provenance from jobs 001/002 remains applicable.
